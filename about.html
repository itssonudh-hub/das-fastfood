About Das Fastfood

A neighbourhood favourite for Chinese and Indo-Chinese fast food.

Das Fastfood serves Chinese and Indo-Chinese fast food the way it should be — cooked fresh when you order, not sitting under a heat lamp. From Manchurian and 65s to noodles, fried rice, and our signature Dum Biryani, every dish is made to order and delivered hot to your door.

We keep things simple: a focused menu, consistent recipes, and free home delivery — no gimmicks, no inflated claims. Just food made the way regulars keep coming back for.
